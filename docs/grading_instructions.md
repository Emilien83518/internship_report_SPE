# Internship Report Grading Instructions

Source: Rapports des stages - Stagiaires régions anglophones
Assignment: Internship report SURFETUD

This document lists the official evaluation criteria used to grade this internship report. Each aspect is scored 0, 1, or 2 points.

## Part 1 — The Overall Structure of the Internship Report

### Aspect 1 — Writing clear and structured deliverables
Good level of language (French or English), no syntactical/lexical/spelling errors, follows instructions.
- **0 pt** — The report is not clear and contains a significant number of errors
- **1 pt** — Good use of the language, no errors in syntax, spelling, or grammar
- **2 pts** — No errors in syntax/spelling/grammar; content is easily understandable by someone unfamiliar with the project (simplification) and summarizes key points in a relevant and simple way

### Aspect 2 — Ability to synthesize and summarize information
- **0 pt** — Lots of redundancies, unstructured report, plagiarism
- **1 pt** — Some redundancies, no plagiarism
- **2 pts** — Good synthesis skills, straight to the point, no redundancies, respects citation rules, no plagiarism

### Aspect 3 — Writing professional deliverables (effectiveness and accuracy, structure and illustrations)
- **0 pt** — Academic and unstructured, no illustrations or inappropriate illustrations
- **1 pt** — Structured but lacks precision and synthesis, some illustrations
- **2 pts** — Professional quality in form and content, efficient/precise/concise, appropriate illustrations

## Part 2 — The Tasks and Different Stages of the Internship

### Aspect 4 — Internship objectives
Objectives clearly stated along with the different tasks.
- **0 pt** — Unable to identify and state objectives and tasks
- **1 pt** — Generally identified objectives but not stated clearly
- **2 pts** — Objectives and tasks clearly identified and stated

### Aspect 5 — Presentation of tasks performed, contextualized within the organization and economic/societal environment
- **0 pt** — Does not present tasks performed
- **1 pt** — Presents tasks without connection to economic/societal environment
- **2 pts** — Presents tasks from a professional perspective, links work to organizational structure and economic/societal environment

### Aspect 6 — Sustainable Development
Company's CSR policy, environmental/social impact of projects, ability to propose improvements.
- **0 pt** — Does not address sustainable development
- **1 pt** — Presents CSR policy, minimal analysis of impacts, no real solutions
- **2 pts** — Presents CSR policy, relevant analysis of impacts, proposes realistic/innovative solutions

### Aspect 7 — Description of techniques/tools used, technical difficulties
- **0 pt** — No techniques/tools or difficulties presented
- **1 pt** — Describes techniques/tools used
- **2 pts** — Describes techniques/tools with understanding of context and mastery of tools

### Aspect 8 — Technical knowledge
Technical, organizational, and/or managerial skills acquired and applied, per professional sector requirements.
- **0 pt** — Did not demonstrate application of knowledge/skills
- **1 pt** — Good technical/organizational/managerial knowledge
- **2 pts** — Excellent knowledge, successfully applied skills acquired during training

### Aspect 9 — Evaluating situations encountered and reflecting to improve
- **0 pt** — Presents situations without reflection or solutions
- **1 pt** — Able to analyze difficulties encountered
- **2 pts** — Analyzes difficulties, reflects on them, draws relevant conclusions to improve

## Part 3 — The Research and Benefits of the Internship

### Aspect 10 — Internship search process
Describe/analyze the search process, identify ways to improve the next search.
- **0 pt** — No description, no improvement ideas
- **1 pt** — Brief description, reflects on improvements
- **2 pts** — Clear analysis of methods/tools used, evaluates potential improvements

### Aspect 11 — Contribution of internship experience to career plan
- **0 pt** — Unable to analyze the contribution
- **1 pt** — Analyzes contribution but doesn't connect to professional project
- **2 pts** — Thoroughly examines the contribution to refine or build career plan

## Part 5 — The Research, Tasks and Contribution of the Internship (Summary)

### Aspect 15 — Internship search process and areas for improvement, clear and concise
- **0 pt** — No description, no improvement areas identified
- **1 pt** — Describes process and suggests improvements, but lacks clarity/conciseness
- **2 pts** — Clear and concise reflection on methods/tools used and areas for improvement

### Aspect 16 — Analysis of how the internship contributed to career plan
- **0 pt** — No analysis, no professional projection
- **1 pt** — Analyzes contribution but doesn't effectively relate it to professional project
- **2 pts** — Clear and relevant analysis to build/refine professional project

### Aspect 17 — Organization chart of the company
- **0 pt** — Does not present the organization
- **1 pt** — Describes organization in a different format
- **2 pts** — Describes organization clearly, legibly, well-presented as an organizational chart

### Aspect 18 — Tasks performed contextualized in graphic form
- **0 pt** — Does not present tasks performed
- **1 pt** — Vaguely presents tasks; graphics present but unclear/inappropriate
- **2 pts** — Clear, relevant, well-structured graphics linking tasks to the organization

### Aspect 19 — Skills used linked to future engineering profession, in graphic form
- **0 pt** — No skills presented, no connection to future profession
- **1 pt** — Partial description, no connection to profession; graphs present but hard to read or poorly selected
- **2 pts** — Precise description, clear connection to future profession, appropriate graphics

## Part 6 — Sustainable Development within the Company

### Aspect 20 — CSR policy, environmental/social impact
- **0 pt** — Does not address sustainable development
- **1 pt** — Partially describes CSR policy
- **2 pts** — Presents CSR policy

### Aspect 21 — Improvement suggestions and overall CSR grade
- **0 pt** — No improvement areas suggested, no grade assigned
- **1 pt** — Proposes real improvement and assigns an appropriate grade
- **2 pts** — Proposes realistic/innovative solutions matching company context, assigns consistent grade

## Part 7 — Conclusion

### Aspect 22 — One-sentence illustrated summary of the experience
The aspect of the professional world that made the greatest impression during the experience.
- **0 pt** — No illustration
- **1 pt** — Trivial or unremarkable representation of the professional world
- **2 pts** — Well-expressed sentence showing authentic personal reflection on the professional world
